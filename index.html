I work on increasingly capable machine systems and the harder question underneath them: how to make capability useful without confusing capability with authority.

Candidates are allowed to be strange. Claims still have to survive contact with evidence.

01 / Field

What this surface is for.

Undomesticated AI is my public research notebook: architectural questions, experiments, external pressure tests, failures worth exposing, and ideas that are not yet ready to be collapsed into products or conventional categories.

The useful boundary is between exploration and admission. Exploration can generate candidates aggressively. Later passes decide what actually survives.

  • Persistent machine systems.
    Software that perceives changing environments, reasons across longer horizons, coordinates work, and remains inspectable.
  • Agent-to-agent infrastructure.
    What changes when machine systems become continuous participants in work rather than tools opened for isolated tasks.
  • Authority architecture.
    How execution rights, operator authorization, resource limits, provenance, and reviewability remain external to model confidence.
  • Discovery machinery.
    How systems can search, mutate, compare, falsify, and preserve promising candidates without promoting speculation into fact.
02 / Discipline

Freedom in discovery. Friction at the boundary.

Useful exploration should not be forced to look sensible too early. Execution is different. Once a candidate crosses into code, communication, deployment, spending, confidential access, or other external effect, evidence and authority matter.

01
Capability is not authority.
A model, agent, or generated artifact does not become governance authority by being competent.
02
Artifacts outrank narrative.
Code, tests, diffs, receipts, logs, schemas, and falsifiers carry more weight than confident description.
03
Failure is part of the record.
Blocked runs, adverse evidence, unknowns, and withheld promotions are not cleaned out of the story.
04
Novelty does not require immediate utility.
Some discoveries should remain seeds until a later Reality Sieve gives them a lawful shape.
03 / ANANS

Where ANANS fits.

ANANS is the active research-and-build programme through which I test many of these questions in software. It is not reduced here to an AI governance tool, compliance product, agent framework, DevSecOps system, or consulting method.

This site is not the ANANS runtime, evidence store, or confidential research surface. When a public statement about ANANS depends on implementation evidence, the intent is to point outward to inspectable public artifacts rather than ask the reader to accept narrative authority.

Public non-claim: ongoing work shown here should not be read as a claim that ANANS is finished, externally validated, production-ready, or generally autonomous.

04 / Notes

No content machine is running behind this page. No automatic ANANS-to-web publication lane exists here. Notes and essays will be added deliberately when they are worth exposing.
