What a digest establishes

A matching SHA-256 establishes consistency with the selected registry. It does not authenticate the registry's author, prove canonical custody, or establish release permission. A locally replaced registry remains operator-supplied material requiring external review. Distinct role identifiers do not prove evaluator independence.
