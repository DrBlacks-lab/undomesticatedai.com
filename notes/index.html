Notes.

Working observations are allowed to remain unresolved. The point is to preserve live candidates without laundering them into claims.

Register
Working proposition

Capability is not authority.

A system can become substantially better at planning, coding, coordination, or communication without gaining any corresponding right to decide what may be executed.

Working proposition

Receipts may be part of the product, not merely audit exhaust.

For consequential agentic work, a reviewer-safe account of what was allowed, refused, changed, tested, and left unknown can be economically and operationally valuable in its own right.

Working proposition

The closest known category can be the wrong category.

Novel systems are often flattened into their nearest training-data neighbor. Comparison is useful for orientation; it becomes destructive when it replaces description of the actual object.

Working proposition

Discovery and admission should be different machines.

Exploration benefits from range, mutation, and strange candidates. Execution benefits from authority checks, falsifiers, provenance, budgets, and fail-closed boundaries. Collapsing the two optimizes neither.

State discipline

Five states stay distinct.

01
Implemented and locally validated.
Code exists and passed the stated local validation.
02
Implemented but not independently proven.
Local evidence exists; external proof does not.
03
Specified but not built.
The design exists. Implementation does not.
04
External claim awaiting receipts.
A potentially useful fact has not yet been admitted.
05
Experimental or speculative candidate.
Worth preserving, not yet worth believing.
