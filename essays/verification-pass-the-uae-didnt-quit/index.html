Corrections required

Correction 1 — Iranian ambassador quote date (HIGH PRIORITY)

Current draft says: “Iran’s ambassador to Pakistan, Reza Amiri Moghadam, addressed Gulf states on April 21, the week before the OPEC announcement.”

Actual date: Tuesday, April 7, 2026 — three weeks before the OPEC announcement, the day before the original ceasefire was announced.

Verbatim quote (CNBC, April 8, 2026): Moghadam warned Gulf states to “pay attention to their conditions and relations with Iran” and that “sooner or later America will leave this region by accepting defeat, and you will stay.”

Source: CNBC, “Gulf countries scramble to intercept missiles hours into U.S.-Iran ceasefire agreement,” April 8, 2026 — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/08/iran-missile-attacks-after-us-ceasefire-gulf-air-defenses.html

Suggested replacement passage:

Iran’s ambassador to Pakistan, Reza Amiri Moghadam, addressed Gulf states on April 7, three weeks before the OPEC announcement. The phrasing matters: pay attention to your conditions and your relations with Iran. Sooner or later America will leave this region by accepting defeat, and you will stay.

The earlier date does not weaken the counter-photon — it strengthens it. The warning was issued before Bessent’s swap-line testimony, before the Iron Dome public revelation, before the Gargash GCC speech. Tehran put the bet on the table first; Abu Dhabi took it anyway.

Correction 2 — Iron Dome deployment timing (MEDIUM PRIORITY)

Current draft says: “Late March. Israel deploys Iron Dome batteries to UAE soil, operated by IDF personnel — believed to be the first foreign deployment of Iron Dome during an active conflict in its history.”

Actual chronology: The Iron Dome deployment happened “early in the war” — per Axios reporting citing Israeli and American officials, this means February–March. What happened on April 26 was the public revelation of the deployment, via Axios scoop, picked up within 24 hours by Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post, Newsweek, Middle East Eye, and Defence Security Asia.

This actually tightens the chronology rather than weakening it: the public disclosure of the unprecedented Israeli-Emirati military integration came two days before the OPEC announcement, not weeks earlier.

Sources:

  • Axios, “Scoop: Israel sent ‘Iron Dome’ system and troops to UAE during Iran war,” April 26, 2026 — https://www.axios.com/2026/04/26/israel-iron-dome-uae
  • Jerusalem Post, “Israel sent Iron Dome system, dozens of IDF soldiers to UAE during Iran war,” April 26, 2026 — https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-894195
  • Times of Israel, April 26, 2026 — https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-sent-iron-dome-and-troops-to-help-defend-uae-during-iran-war-report/
  • Newsweek — https://www.newsweek.com/israel-give-united-arab-emirates-iron-dome-11880635

Suggested replacement passage:

April 26. Axios reveals what Israeli and American officials had not previously disclosed: early in the war, Israel deployed an Iron Dome battery and several dozen IDF operators to UAE soil after a direct call between Netanyahu and Mohamed bin Zayed. The system intercepted dozens of Iranian missiles. It was the first time Israel had sent an Iron Dome battery to another country, and the UAE was the first nation outside Israel and the United States to operate the system in active combat. A senior Emirati official, quoted in the Axios piece: “We are not going to forget it.”
