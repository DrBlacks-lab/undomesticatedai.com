Non-claim

Work in public without pretending the work is finished.

Nothing on this site should be read as evidence that ANANS is finished, externally validated, production-ready, or generally autonomous. A working implementation remains a working implementation. A synthetic demonstration remains synthetic. An external claim remains external until source-bound.

The publication can move fast at the level of ideas while staying conservative about what has actually been proven.
